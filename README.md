## 📖 About

X4: Foundations by Egosoft (2018) is the most comprehensive space simulation ever made. You build an economic empire across a living galaxy, managing fleets, factories, and trade routes while engaging in real-time dogfights. Its depth is astounding but its learning curve is near-vertical.

## ⚠️ Problems Without Trainer

| Problem | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Station construction requires complex supply chain setup before generating income | ✅ Solved |
| Fleet management interface requires significant learning to avoid inefficiencies | ✅ Solved |
| Xenon attacks destroy undefended stations in overnight play sessions | ✅ Solved |
| Plot mission trigger conditions obscure requiring wiki knowledge to advance | ✅ Solved |
| Pilot training progression requires hundreds of hours to reach efficient levels | ✅ Solved |

## ✅ Solution

The **X4: Foundations Trainer** solves all these issues, giving you complete control so you can focus on enjoying the game.
